Eldingo! in sans-serif

Option A, the lightbulb, set in twelve sans-serif faces. Each row shows the logo at full size, then at header size with a favicon so you can judge legibility where it will mostly live. The bulb lights on load; hover to spin the rays. All are free Google Fonts, so whichever you pick becomes the site's heading font too.
