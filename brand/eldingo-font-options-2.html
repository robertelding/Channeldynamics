Eldingo! in Helvetica-style and sketchy faces

Two families. First, Helvetica Neue and the free grotesques closest to it, all with the red wordmark and the yellow burst. Second, hand-drawn faces with a sketched bulb that draws itself on load. Hover any wordmark to replay the animation; reload to see the load sequence.

1 · Helvetica Neue and clean grotesques

Helvetica Neue is shown as rendered by your Mac. For the website we would license it or use Inter or Hanken Grotesk, which are almost indistinguishable at these weights.
