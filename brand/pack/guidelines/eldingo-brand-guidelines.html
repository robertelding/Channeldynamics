Brand guidelines

Version 1.0 · October 2026

Eldingo is a trading name of MyStoryboarder Ltd. These guidelines cover the logo, colour, type, motion and stationery, and index every file in the brand pack.

The idea

Eldingo! is the moment an idea makes sense. The exclamation mark is a cord with a bulb hanging from it. Everything in the identity comes from that: a confident sans-serif wordmark, one warm accent that only ever appears as the light, and a single, gentle animation.

The logo

Navy is the primary logo on white or light backgrounds. White is reversed out of navy for footers, dark slides and social cards. Red is reserved for Red Team and Switch On material delivered with redfirst.ai; never use it as the general logo.

Construction

The wordmark is set in Helvetica Neue Bold with 3.5% negative tracking and supplied as outlines, so no font is needed to reproduce it. The bulb is always spark yellow; the stroke, collar and base take the wordmark colour. Do not redraw, retype or recolour the bulb.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the bulb on every side. Minimum width: 24 mm in print, 120 px on screen. Below that, use the bulb mark alone.

The bulb mark

The exclamation mark on its own: for favicons, app icons, social avatars, section markers and as a decorative element on innovation content. The favicon drops the cord and keeps the bulb and collar.

Colour

White space first. Navy carries the message, yellow is the light, red appears only for Red Team work.

Rules

  • Yellow is never used for body text and never fills a whole page.
  • Navy on white and white on navy both pass WCAG AA at every size. Yellow buttons carry navy text.
  • No dark-mode pages. Dark bands are navy, used sparingly for stats, quotes, calls to action and the footer.

Typography

Logo: Helvetica Neue Bold (outlined in the files). Website and documents: Inter, a free Google Font with the same proportions, in weights 400 to 800. Fallbacks: Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial.

Voice

Short sentences, plain English, a little dry humour. The exclamation mark in the logo is the only one in the brand; the copy never shouts. Say what we did, what the client reported, and never invent a number.

Motion

One signature move, used sparingly.

  • At rest: the halo behind the bulb pulses slowly and the bulb sways about two degrees, as if hanging on its cord.
  • On load: the bulb starts grey, flickers twice and lights.
  • On hover: a quick flicker and one swing.
  • Never: sunbursts, spinning rays, looping flashes, or more than one animated logo in view. Respect "reduce motion" settings: the files already do.

Files: the animated SVGs play in any modern browser and in email clients that support SVG; the MP4 is for video intros and social; the GIF is for places that allow neither.

Business card

85 × 55 mm trim with 3 mm bleed (files are 91 × 61 mm), 5 mm safe area. Front: navy with the white logo and the three-word descriptor. Back: white with name, title, contact details, the bulb mark and the legal line. Print on 350 to 400 gsm uncoated or silk stock; a spot-UV bulb on the front works well. Supplied as vector PDF for print, 600 dpi PNG for proofing, and editable SVG.

Do and don't

Do

  • Use the supplied files; never retype the wordmark.
  • Put the navy logo on white or mist, the white logo on navy.
  • Keep the bulb yellow in every version.
  • Leave generous space around it.

Don't

  • Recolour, outline, add effects or rotate the logo.
  • Place it on photographs or busy backgrounds.
  • Use the red logo outside Red Team material.
  • Pair it with a second exclamation mark in copy.
