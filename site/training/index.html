Training FAQs

How do I book a training course?

Email us at hello@channeldynamics.io or use the contact page and we will confirm availability, dates and payment details.

Is the training right for me?

We assume some awareness of the user experience field. The courses are appropriate for anyone working in the creation of digital products and want to learn about the process, tools and techniques to create successful user experiences.

Do I have to take the courses all together or can I spread them out?

Courses can be taken over an 18-month period. If you register for the full certification, there is some discount so it works out cheaper than the individual courses when added up.

What is the latest I can book the course?

Registration will stay open until the day before the course starts or a maximum number of students have booked (16).

When do you post your training schedule of upcoming courses?

We post our course schedule at least three months in advance where possible. This will be listed on the website. Please register if you would like to be notified of upcoming courses via email (see the sign-up form on this page).

How can I pay?

Courses and Certification programs can be paid through credit or debit card at online registration. We can issue an invoice statement on request in addition to the pdf confirmation sent out as part of the payment confirmation. (If you require an invoice to be sent for payment, please email hello@channeldynamics.io). We do not accept cheques at this time.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please contact us for our current cancellation terms.

Do you offer any discounts?

We offer a student discount of 10% off individual courses. A valid student ID is required. A group discount of 10% is also available when three or more from the same organisation register for the same courses. Please contact hello@channeldynamics.io to discuss options.

Are there any other costs apart from the course fees?

No, the course fees include training materials and lunch. Any other personal costs such as travel or accommodation will need to be covered by the attendees.

Are your courses CPD certified?

Yes, our courses are certified for CPD by the CPD Accreditation organisation.

The Exam and related details

If you have completed the required courses, and when you are ready to take the exam. Please let us know the date you would like to sit the exam. We will verify your attendance at each course before adding you to the exam session. You cannot register for the exam without attending all of the courses. If you fail the exam: If you fail the certification exam, you can retake the exam with the next session. All exams are supervised by a Channel Dynamics representative and must be completed in person. We do not offer remote or online exams. Exam Results: Each certification requires a pass grade of 70% or more on the final test. Results will be assessed and sent approximately two weeks after the exam date.

Do I need to bring anything with me to the training course?

A laptop is recommended for the Visual Design, Information Architecture and Usability Testing courses. It would be useful if you had relevant design packages installed such as Sketch and In-Vision. Channel Dynamics will provide the relevant workshop materials such as pens and Post-It notes etc.

Do you offer customised or In-house training?

Yes, we offer a range of in-house training options based on any of our courses and can also create bespoke courses if required for additional fees.

Is the Channel Dynamics certification recognised worldwide?

Channel Dynamics UX training courses are CPD recognised certifications for UX practitioners and can be used worldwide. We ensure that materials are current and that our instructors are qualified to lead training in UX design related activities.

Interested in training for your team?

Tell us what you’d like your team to learn and we’ll put together a course outline and quote.
